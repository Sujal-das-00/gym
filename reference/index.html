How it works

Three steps to a paperless gym

Sign up today, and take your first check-in the same evening.

Create your gym

Sign up with your gym's name and your email. Your dashboard and your owner login are ready straight away.

Add your members

Add members with their plan, fee and start date. Everyone gets a Gym ID and a check-in QR code automatically.

Share the check-in link

Put the link or QR code at the front desk. Members install it as an app and check themselves in from then on.

For your members

An app your members actually keep

The check-in page installs on a phone like any other app — no app store, no download, nothing for you to maintain.
