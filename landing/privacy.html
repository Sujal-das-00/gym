1. Who we are

Gymboo ("we", "us") provides gym-management software: member records, check-in, fee tracking, expenses and notifications. Our apps and website are available at https://gymbot.toolszila.com.

There are two roles to understand:

  • The gym (your gym owner or staff) decides which member details to record and why. For member data, the gym is the "data controller" (data fiduciary).
  • Gymboo stores and processes that data on the gym's behalf, and is the controller of the gym-owner and referral-partner accounts we create ourselves.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone recordings or health data.

3. How we use it

  • Provide the service: sign-in, member and fee records, check-in and attendance.
  • Send notifications your gym chooses to send (announcements, fee and membership reminders). You can turn them off.
  • Keep the service secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and fix bugs.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to your requests.
  • Pay referral partners.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or to train AI models, and we never sell it.

5. Who we share it with

  • Your gym. Gym owners and their staff see their own members' data. Members of one gym can never see another gym's data.
  • Service providers who help us operate: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivers push notifications; receives your notification token and message) and our hosting and database provider. They may process data only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, court order or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business change. If Gymboo is merged or acquired, data may transfer under this same policy.

We do not sell or rent personal data.

6. How long we keep it

  • We keep data while your account or your gym's account is active.
  • When a gym removes a member, the record is archived (hidden from normal use) so fee history stays accurate; it is permanently deleted on request or when the gym's account is closed.
  • When you ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example billing records), which are kept only as long as required.
  • Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS encryption. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Sign-in uses secure session cookies, logins are rate-limited, uploaded images are re-encoded and each gym's data is separated from other gyms. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will notify affected users and authorities where the law requires.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws), you can:

  • Ask what data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate data (members can also ask their gym).
  • Delete your data or account: see how to delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent, such as turning off notifications or removing your photo.
  • Complain to us, or to your local data-protection authority.

Members: because your gym controls your membership record, we may refer your request to them, or act on it ourselves if they do not respond.

9. Children

Our apps are intended for people aged 18 and over. Gyms may enrol a minor only with a parent or guardian's consent, and the parent or guardian is responsible for that record. We do not knowingly collect data from children through our own accounts; contact us if you believe we have and we will delete it.

10. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers operated by our hosting provider and may be processed in countries other than yours, including India and the countries where Google operates. We use providers that protect data to a reasonable standard.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email. Continuing to use the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
