Gymboo Privacy Policy

Delete your account and data

For the Gym Boo and Gym Boo Member apps by Gymboo.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@gymboo.app with the subject "Delete my Gymboo account".
  2. Send it from the email address on your account (gym owners and partners), or include your name, phone number and gym name (members) so we can find your record.
  3. We may reply to confirm it is really you. This protects your account from being deleted by someone else.
  4. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Account typeDeleted
MemberYour name, phone number, address, photo, attendance and check-in history, payment records, and notification tokens.
Gym ownerYour account, your gym and all of its members, trainers, attendance, payments, expenses and settings. This cannot be undone, so export anything you need first.
Referral partnerYour profile, login and referral code.

What we may keep

Records we are legally required to keep (for example invoices, licence-purchase and payout records for tax and accounting) may be kept for the legally required period, then deleted. Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle. We do not use retained data for any other purpose.

Just want to stop notifications or remove your photo?

  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's Settings → Apps → Gym Boo Member → Notifications.
  • Photo or details: ask your gym to change them, or email us.

Members: your gym also holds your membership record. If you only want to leave the gym, tell the gym; if you want your data erased, email us as above.
