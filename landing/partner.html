Eligibility & rules

Who can join, and the ground rules

Simple and fair. Read these once and you know exactly how you earn.

Who can join

  • Anyone with an email address and a phone number. No gym required.
  • Great for trainers, fitness consultants, equipment sellers, gym staff, creators and friends of gym owners.
  • The gym you refer must be new to Gymboo. A gym that has already bought a licence cannot be tagged to a code.

What counts as a successful referral

  • The gym enters your code while registering. If they forgot, they can message us before buying and we can add it.
  • The gym buys the one-time licence and we approve the purchase.
  • Each gym can earn you a reward once. There is no reward for renewals or repeat purchases.
  • The monthly plan does not qualify, and does not use up the referral. If the gym buys the one-time licence later, it still counts.

What isn't allowed

  • Using your own code for a gym you own or run, or one registered with your own email.
  • Fake or duplicate sign-ups made only to collect rewards.
  • Misleading gym owners about price or features.
  • We may pause an account and hold rewards while we look into suspected misuse.

FAQ

Partner questions, answered

When can I withdraw my balance?

As soon as you have a balance. Your balance is credited the moment we approve the referred gym's one-time licence purchase, and from then on you can request a payout whenever you like. There is no minimum amount and no waiting period.

How long does a payout take?

We settle payout requests within 24 hours. The request shows as Processing in your dashboard and changes to Paid once the money is sent. If you want to follow up, message us at support@gymboo.app.

Why does my referral say Pending?

Pending means the gym registered with your code but hasn't bought the one-time licence yet, or we haven't approved the purchase yet. It becomes Successful, and your reward is added, after approval. A pending referral doesn't expire.

Do monthly plans earn a reward?

No. Only the one-time licence earns the ₹1,000 reward, and only the one-time licence gets the ₹2,000 discount. If a referred gym starts on the monthly plan and buys the one-time licence later, your referral still counts then.

Is the reward paid more than once for the same gym?

No. Each referred gym earns a reward once, on its first one-time licence purchase. Refer more gyms to keep earning, as there is no limit.

What if the gym forgets to enter my code?

The easiest way is to send your share link. It opens the registration page with your code already filled in. If a gym still forgot, ask them to message us before they buy and we can attach your code to their account. Once a referral has been rewarded it can't be changed.

Can I use my own code?

No. A code can't be used for a gym registered with the same email as the partner account. The programme is for introducing new gyms to Gymboo.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free, and there are no fees on your earnings or payouts.

Where do I see my earnings?

In your partner dashboard: total referrals, successful referrals, total earned, available balance, every referred gym with its status, and your payout history. Log in on this page any time.

How do I join?

Fill in your name, email, phone and a password in the form at the top of this page. Your referral code is created immediately.
