FAQ

Questions gym owners ask

How much does Gymboo cost?

You can get started for free. After that, choose one of two plans. The one-time plan is ₹6,999 for the licence (₹1,000 off the regular ₹7,999), with server and maintenance free in the first year and ₹1,299 a year from year two. The monthly plan is ₹249 a month with everything included.

Do my members need an app store?

No. Members open your gym's check-in link and install it to their home screen from the browser, like any other app.

How do members check in?

They scan the check-in QR code at your front desk, or open the app they installed. Attendance is recorded automatically.

Can my front-desk staff use it?

Yes. As the owner you can add staff logins, which have more limited access than yours.

What is included in both plans?

A desktop app, a mobile app for the admin, a branded member app, the setup of your gym, plus server and maintenance: free in the first year of the one-time plan, then ₹1,299 a year. The monthly plan includes all of it.

Who owns my members' data?

You do. You can export your records as CSV from the dashboard whenever you want.

Will it work the way my gym works?

Yes. You choose how check-in works for memberships, how your billing cycle runs, when fees are collected and which days you are closed.

Is there a referral programme?

Yes. Join free as a partner and share your code. A gym that registers with it saves ₹2,000 on the one-time licence, and you earn ₹1,000 once their purchase is approved. See how it works.

How do I get started?

Register your gym with its name and your email. Your dashboard and owner login are created straight away, and you can add your first member right after.
